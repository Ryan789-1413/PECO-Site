Our in-house laboratory

Ridgewater AnalyticalFull-capability water and chemistry lab

Most treatment companies send samples out and wait. Our expanded analytical laboratory, Ridgewater Analytical, gives Power Engineering on-site support to identify, define and fix problems quickly. We can quantify what is in your water, confirm that our products perform, and act on the data without delay.

  • LeadershipIn-house PhD chemist
  • InstrumentsHPLC, ICP and IC: a complete in-house analytical suite for every analysis we run
  • FunctionsQuality control, research and development, advanced analytical testing
  • LocationSalt Lake City, alongside our chemical production
High-performance liquid chromatography HPLC

Separates and quantifies organic compounds, so we can measure the active ingredients that protect your system.

  • Product QC and active-ingredient verification
  • Azole copper-corrosion inhibitors
  • Formulation research and development
Inductively coupled plasma ICP

Multi-element metals analysis down to trace levels, the clearest view of scaling and corrosion activity.

  • Calcium and magnesium hardness
  • Iron, copper and zinc corrosion indicators
  • Silica, molybdenum and total phosphorus
Ion chromatography IC

Precise anion and cation profiling for makeup water characterization and cycles verification.

  • Chloride, sulfate and nitrate
  • Orthophosphate and fluoride
  • Nitrite levels in closed loops

About us

Eighty years of keeping water working.

Power Engineering began in 1946 as a design-build firm for steam generating plants. In 1961 we turned that plant-side experience to water treatment, and we have designed, installed and maintained treatment systems and programs across the Mountain West ever since.

Over the decades we have grown into a complete industrial water ecosystem. We consult with design engineers during planning, track regional water supply patterns to customize each program, and work with a university metallurgical department on corrosion questions. We manufacture our own chemical formulations, fabricate our own feed equipment, operate our own analytical laboratory through Ridgewater Analytical, and deliver and service every program with our own team. When each part of the program comes from one place, quality is easier to control and problems are faster to solve.

Our 22 employees work from our Salt Lake City headquarters and our Idaho Falls location, which keeps a local representative and chemical inventory close to customers in Idaho. We remain privately held and independent, and we are active members of the Association of Water Technologies and Associated Labs, which keep our equipment, chemistry and best practices aligned with current industry standards.
